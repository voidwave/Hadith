	<div class="bookintro"><div class=ebookintro>The statement of Allah Most High "O you who have believed, when you rise to [perform] prayer, wash your faces and your forearms to the elbows and wipe over your heads and wash your feet to the ankles. And if you are in a state of janabah, then purify yourselves. But if you are ill or on a journey or one of you comes from the place of relieving himself or you have contacted women and do not find water, then seek clean earth and wipe over your faces and hands with it. Allah does not intend to make difficulty for you, but He intends to purify you and complete His favor upon you that you may be grateful."
<br>
And His statement: "O you who have believed, do not approach prayer while you are intoxicated until you know what you are saying or in a state of janabah, except those passing through [a place of prayer], until you have washed [your whole body]. And if you are ill or on a journey or one of you comes from the place of relieving himself or you have contacted women and find no water, then seek clean earth and wipe over your faces and your hands [with it]. Indeed, Allah is ever Pardoning and Forgiving."</div><div class="arabic abookintro">وَقَوْلِ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى ‏{‏وَإِنْ كُنْتُمْ جُنُبًا فَاطَّهَّرُوا وَإِنْ كُنْتُمْ مَرْضَى أَوْ عَلَى سَفَرٍ أَوْ جَاءَ أَحَدٌ مِنْكُمْ مِنَ الْغَائِطِ أَوْ لاَمَسْتُمُ النِّسَاءَ فَلَمْ تَجِدُوا مَاءً فَتَيَمَّمُوا صَعِيدًا طَيِّبًا فَامْسَحُوا بِوُجُوهِكُمْ وَأَيْدِيكُمْ مِنْهُ مَا يُرِيدُ اللَّهُ لِيَجْعَلَ عَلَيْكُمْ مِنْ حَرَجٍ وَلَكِنْ يُرِيدُ لِيُطَهِّرَكُمْ وَلِيُتِمَّ نِعْمَتَهُ عَلَيْكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَشْكُرُونَ‏}‏ وَقَوْلِهِ جَلَّ ذِكْرُهُ ‏{‏يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لاَ تَقْرَبُوا الصَّلاَةَ وَأَنْتُمْ سُكَارَى حَتَّى تَعْلَمُوا مَا تَقُولُونَ وَلاَ جُنُبًا إِلاَّ عَابِرِي سَبِيلٍ حَتَّى تَغْتَسِلُوا وَإِنْ كُنْتُمْ مَرْضَى أَوْ عَلَى سَفَرٍ أَوْ جَاءَ أَحَدٌ مِنْكُمْ مِنَ الْغَائِطِ أَوْ لاَمَسْتُمُ النِّسَاءَ فَلَمْ تَجِدُوا مَاءً فَتَيَمَّمُوا صَعِيدًا طَيِّبًا فَامْسَحُوا بِوُجُوهِكُمْ وَأَيْدِيكُمْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَانَ عَفُوًّا غَفُورًا‏}‏‏.‏</div><div class=clear></div></div>

<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 248</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102490><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102490><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Narrated `Aisha:</div><div class=text_details><p>Whenever the Prophet (ﷺ) took a bath after Janaba he started by washing his hands and then performed

<a name=4></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 251</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102520><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102520><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>

<div class=echapno>(20)</div><div class=englishchapter>Chapter: Whosoever took a bath alone (in seclusion) completely naked</div>
<div class=achapno>(20)</div>
<div class="arabicchapter arabic">باب مَنِ اغْتَسَلَ عُرْيَانًا وَحْدَهُ فِي الْخَلْوَةِ، وَمَنْ تَسَتَّرَ فَالتَّسَتُّرُ أَفْضَلُ</div><div class=clear></div>
</div>
<div class="arabic achapintro aconly">وَقَالَ بَهْزٌ عَنْ أَبِيهِ عَنْ جَدِّهِ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ: «اللَّهُ أَحَقُّ أَنْ يُسْتَحْيَا مِنْهُ مِنَ النَّاسِ».</div>

<!-- Begin hadith -->

<a name=30></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 278</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102790><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102790><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Narrated Abu Huraira:</div><div class=text_details><p>The Prophet (ﷺ) said, 'The (people of) Bani Israel used to take bath naked (all together) looking at each 
other. The Prophet (ﷺ) Moses used to take a bath alone. They said, 'By Allah! Nothing prevents Moses 
from taking a bath with us except that he has a scrotal hernia.' So once Moses went out to take a bath 
and put his clothes over a stone and then that stone ran away with his clothes. Moses followed that 
stone saying, "My clothes, O stone! My clothes, O stone! till the people of Bani Israel saw him and 
said, 'By Allah, Moses has got no defect in his body. Moses took his clothes and began to beat the 
stone." Abu Huraira added, "By Allah! There are still six or seven marks present on the stone from 
that excessive beating."</b></div>
<div class=clear></div></div></div><div class="arabic_hadith_full arabic"><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">حَدَّثَنَا إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ نَصْرٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، عَنْ مَعْمَرٍ، عَنْ هَمَّامِ بْنِ مُنَبِّهٍ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏</span>
<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ كَانَتْ بَنُو إِسْرَائِيلَ يَغْتَسِلُونَ عُرَاةً، يَنْظُرُ بَعْضُهُمْ إِلَى بَعْضٍ، وَكَانَ مُوسَى يَغْتَسِلُ وَحْدَهُ، فَقَالُوا وَاللَّهِ مَا يَمْنَعُ مُوسَى أَنْ يَغْتَسِلَ مَعَنَا إِلاَّ أَنَّهُ آدَرُ، فَذَهَبَ مَرَّةً يَغْتَسِلُ، فَوَضَعَ ثَوْبَهُ عَلَى حَجَرٍ، فَفَرَّ الْحَجَرُ بِثَوْبِهِ، فَخَرَجَ مُوسَى فِي إِثْرِهِ يَقُولُ ثَوْبِي يَا حَجَرُ‏.‏ حَتَّى نَظَرَتْ بَنُو إِسْرَائِيلَ إِلَى مُوسَى، فَقَالُوا وَاللَّهِ مَا بِمُوسَى مِنْ بَأْسٍ‏.‏ وَأَخَذَ ثَوْبَهُ، فَطَفِقَ بِالْحَجَرِ ضَرْبًا ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ وَاللَّهِ إِنَّهُ لَنَدَبٌ بِالْحَجَرِ سِتَّةٌ أَوْ سَبْعَةٌ ضَرْبًا بِالْحَجَرِ‏.‏</span></div>
</div>

<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 280</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102810><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102810><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>

<a name=33></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 281</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102820><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102820><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Narrated Maimuna:</div><div class=text_details><p>I screened the Prophet (ﷺ) while he was taking a bath of Janaba. He washed his hands, poured water from 
his right hand over his left and washed his private parts. Then he rubbed his hand over a wall or the 
earth, and performed ablution similar to that for the prayer but did not wash his feet. Then he poured 
water over his body, shifted from that place, and washed his feet.</b></div>

<a name=34></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 282</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102830><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102830><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Narrated Um-Salama:</div><div class=text_details><p>(the mother of the believers) Um Sulaim, the wife of Abu Talha, came to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and said, "O 
Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)! Verily Allah is not shy of (telling you) the truth. Is it necessary for a woman to take a

notices a discharge."</b></div>

<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ نَعَمْ إِذَا رَأَتِ الْمَاءَ ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏‏.‏</span></div>

<a name=35></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 283</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102840><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102840><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Narrated Abu Huraira:</div><div class=text_details><p>The Prophet (ﷺ) came across me in one of the streets of Medina and at that time I was Junub. So I slipped 
away from him and went to take a bath. On my return the Prophet (ﷺ) said, "O Abu Huraira! Where have 
you been?" I replied, "I was Junub, so I disliked to sit in your company." The Prophet (ﷺ) said, "Subhan

<a name=44></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 292</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102930><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102930><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Narrated Zaid bin Khalid Al-Juhani:</div><div class=text_details><p>I asked `Uthman bin `Affan about a man who engaged in the sexual intercourse with his wife but did 
not discharge. `Uthman replied, "He should perform ablution like that for the prayer after washing his 
private parts." `Uthman added, "I heard that from Allah's Messenger (ﷺ)." I asked `Ali bin Abi Talib, Az- 
Zubair bin Al-`Awwam, Talha bin 'Ubaidullah and Ubai bin Ka`b and they gave the same reply. (Abu 
Aiyub said that he had heard that from Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) ) (This order was canceled later on so one has 
to take a bath. See, Hadith No. 180).</b></div>
<div class=clear></div></div></div><div class="arabic_hadith_full arabic"><span class="arabic_sanad arabic"></span>
<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، عَنِ الْحُسَيْنِ، قَالَ يَحْيَى وَأَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ، أَنَّ عَطَاءَ بْنَ يَسَارٍ، أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّ زَيْدَ بْنَ خَالِدٍ الْجُهَنِيَّ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ، سَأَلَ عُثْمَانَ بْنَ عَفَّانَ فَقَالَ أَرَأَيْتَ إِذَا جَامَعَ الرَّجُلُ امْرَأَتَهُ فَلَمْ يُمْنِ‏.‏ قَالَ عُثْمَانُ يَتَوَضَّأُ كَمَا يَتَوَضَّأُ لِلصَّلاَةِ، وَيَغْسِلُ ذَكَرَهُ‏.‏ قَالَ عُثْمَانُ سَمِعْتُهُ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏ فَسَأَلْتُ عَنْ ذَلِكَ عَلِيَّ بْنَ أَبِي طَالِبٍ وَالزُّبَيْرَ بْنَ الْعَوَّامِ وَطَلْحَةَ بْنَ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ وَأُبَىَّ بْنَ كَعْبٍ ـ رضى الله عنهم ـ فَأَمَرُوهُ بِذَلِكَ‏.‏ قَالَ يَحْيَى وَأَخْبَرَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ أَنَّ عُرْوَةَ بْنَ الزُّبَيْرِ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّ أَبَا أَيُّوبَ أَخْبَرَهُ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ ذَلِكَ مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic"></span></div>
</div>

<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 293</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc102940><div class="englishcontainer" id=t102940><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
