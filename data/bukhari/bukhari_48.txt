<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ مَا أَصْبَحَ لآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلاَّ صَاعٌ، وَلاَ أَمْسَى ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏‏.‏ وَإِنَّهُمْ لَتِسْعَةُ أَبْيَاتٍ‏.‏</span></div>

<a name=3></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih al-Bukhari 2510</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc124260><div class="englishcontainer" id=t124260><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:</div><div class=text_details><p>Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) said, "Who would kill Ka`b bin Al-Ashraf as he has harmed Allah and His Apostle ?" 
Muhammad bin Maslama (got up and) said, "I will kill him." So, Muhammad bin Maslama went to 
Ka`b and said, "I want a loan of one or two Wasqs of food grains." Ka`b said, "Mortgage your women 
to me." Muhammad bin Maslama said, "How can we mortgage our women, and you are the most 
handsome among the Arabs?" He said, "Then mortgage your sons to me." Muhammad said, "How can 
we mortgage our sons, as the people will abuse them for being mortgaged for one or two Wasqs of 
food grains? It is shameful for us. But we will mortgage our arms to you." So, Muhammad bin 
Maslama promised him that he would come to him next time. They (Muhammad bin Maslama and his 
companions came to him as promised and murdered him. Then they went to the Prophet (ﷺ) and told him 
about it.</b></div>
<div class=clear></div></div></div><div class="arabic_hadith_full arabic"><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، قَالَ عَمْرٌو سَمِعْتُ جَابِرَ بْنَ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ يَقُولُ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏</span>
<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ مَنْ لِكَعْبِ بْنِ الأَشْرَفِ فَإِنَّهُ آذَى اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ أَنَا‏.‏ فَأَتَاهُ فَقَالَ أَرَدْنَا أَنْ تُسْلِفَنَا وَسْقًا أَوْ وَسْقَيْنِ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ارْهَنُونِي نِسَاءَكُمْ‏.‏ قَالُوا كَيْفَ نَرْهَنُكَ نِسَاءَنَا، وَأَنْتَ أَجْمَلُ الْعَرَبِ قَالَ فَارْهَنُونِي أَبْنَاءَكُمْ‏.‏ قَالُوا كَيْفَ نَرْهَنُ أَبْنَاءَنَا فَيُسَبُّ أَحَدُهُمْ، فَيُقَالُ رُهِنَ بِوَسْقٍ أَوْ وَسْقَيْنِ هَذَا عَارٌ عَلَيْنَا وَلَكِنَّا نَرْهَنُكَ اللأْمَةَ ـ قَالَ سُفْيَانُ يَعْنِي السِّلاَحَ ـ فَوَعَدَهُ أَنْ يَأْتِيَهُ فَقَتَلُوهُ، ثُمَّ أَتَوُا النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأَخْبَرُوهُ‏.‏</span></div>
</div>
