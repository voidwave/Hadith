<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih Muslim 2246 c</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc360380><div class="englishcontainer" id=t360380><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Abu Huraira reported Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) as saying:</div><div class=text_details>Allah, the Exalted and Glorious, said: The son of Adam causes Me pain as he says: Woe be upon the Time. None of you should say this: Woe be upon the Time, as I am the Time (because) I alternate the day and the night, and when I wish I can finish them up.</b></div>
<div class=clear></div></div></div><div class="arabic_hadith_full arabic"><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">وَحَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ بْنُ حُمَيْدٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنِ ابْنِ،
 الْمُسَيَّبِ عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏</span>
<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ قَالَ اللَّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ
 يُؤْذِينِي ابْنُ آدَمَ يَقُولُ يَا خَيْبَةَ الدَّهْرِ ‏.‏ فَلاَ يَقُولَنَّ أَحَدُكُمْ يَا خَيْبَةَ الدَّهْرِ ‏.‏ فَإِنِّي أَنَا الدَّهْرُ
 أُقَلِّبُ لَيْلَهُ وَنَهَارَهُ فَإِذَا شِئْتُ قَبَضْتُهُمَا ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏ ‏.‏</span></div>

<a name=20></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih Muslim 2252 a</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc360550><div class="englishcontainer" id=t360550><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Abd Sa'id Khudri reported Allah's Apostle (ﷺ) as saying:</div><div class=text_details>There was a woman from Bani Isra'il who was short-statured and she walked in the company of two tall women with wooden sandals in her feet and a ring of gold made of plates with musk filled in them and then looked up, and musk is the best of scents; then she walked between two women and they (the people) did not recognise her, and she made a gesture with her hand like this, and Shu'ba shook his hand in order to give an indication how she shook her hand.</b></div>
<div class=clear></div></div></div><div class="arabic_hadith_full arabic"><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أُسَامَةَ، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنِي خُلَيْدُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ،
 عَنْ أَبِي نَضْرَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي سَعِيدٍ الْخُدْرِيِّ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏</span>
<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ كَانَتِ امْرَأَةٌ
 مِنْ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ قَصِيرَةٌ تَمْشِي مَعَ امْرَأَتَيْنِ طَوِيلَتَيْنِ فَاتَّخَذَتْ رِجْلَيْنِ مِنْ خَشَبٍ وَخَاتَمًا

 فَقَالَتْ بِيَدِهَا هَكَذَا ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏ ‏.‏ وَنَفَضَ شُعْبَةُ يَدَهُ ‏.‏</span></div>
