<a name=6></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih Muslim 1616 a</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc342580><div class="englishcontainer" id=t342580><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Jabir b. 'Abdullah (Allah be pleased with them) reported:</div><div class=text_details>I fell sick and there came to me on foot Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) and Abu Bakr for inquiring after my health. I fainted. He (the Holy Prophet) performed ablution and then sprinkled over me the water of his ablution. I felt some relief and said: Allah's Messenger, how should I decide about my property? <div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih Muslim 1619 a</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc342710><div class="englishcontainer" id=t342710><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) reported that when the body of a dead person having burden of debt upon him was brought to Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) he would ask whether he had left property enough to clear off his debt, and if the property left had been sufficient for that (purpose), he observed funeral prayer for him, otherwise he said (to his companions):</div><div class=text_details>You observe prayer for your companion. Abn Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) reported Allah's Apostle (ﷺ) having said this:</div><div class=text_details>By Him in Whose Hand is the life of Muhammad, there is no believer on the earth with whom I am not the nearest among all the people. He who amongst you (dies) and leaves a debt, I am there to pay it, and he who amongst you (dies) leaving behind children I am there to look after them. And he who amongst You leaves behind property, that is for the inheritor whoever he is.</b></div>
<div class=clear></div></div></div><div class="arabic_hadith_full arabic"><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ رَافِعٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شَبَابَةُ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي وَرْقَاءُ، عَنْ أَبِي الزِّنَادِ، عَنِ الأَعْرَجِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏</span>
<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ وَالَّذِي نَفْسُ مُحَمَّدٍ بِيَدِهِ إِنْ عَلَى الأَرْضِ مِنْ مُؤْمِنٍ إِلاَّ أَنَا أَوْلَى النَّاسِ بِهِ فَأَيُّكُمْ مَا تَرَكَ دَيْنًا أَوْ ضَيَاعًا فَأَنَا مَوْلاَهُ وَأَيُّكُمْ تَرَكَ مَالاً فَإِلَى الْعَصَبَةِ مَنْ كَانَ ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏ ‏.‏</span></div>
</div>

<a name=21></a>
<div class="hadith_reference_sticky">Sahih Muslim 1619 d</div><div class="hadithTextContainers" id=htc342740><div class="englishcontainer" id=t342740><div class="english_hadith_full"><div class=hadith_narrated><p>
Hammam b. Munabbih reported:</div><div class=text_details>This is what Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) narrated to us from Allah's Messenger (may peace he upon him). And he narrated many ahadith, and one was this: Allah's Messenger (may peace be upon him said: I am, according to the Book of Allah, the Exalted and Majestic, nearest to the believers of all the human beings. So whoever amongst you dies in debt or leaves behind destitute children, you should call me (for help) ), for I am his guardian. And who amongst you leaves property, his inheritor is entitled to get it, whoever he is.</b></div>
<div class=clear></div></div></div><div class="arabic_hadith_full arabic"><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ رَافِعٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّزَّاقِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنْ هَمَّامِ بْنِ مُنَبِّهٍ، قَالَ هَذَا مَا حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو هُرَيْرَةَ، عَنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَذَكَرَ أَحَادِيثَ مِنْهَا وَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏</span>
<span class="arabic_text_details arabic">"‏ أَنَا أَوْلَى النَّاسِ بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ فِي كِتَابِ اللَّهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ فَأَيُّكُمْ مَا تَرَكَ دَيْنًا أَوْ ضَيْعَةً فَادْعُونِي فَأَنَا وَلِيُّهُ وَأَيُّكُمْ مَا تَرَكَ مَالاً فَلْيُؤْثَرْ بِمَالِهِ عَصَبَتُهُ مَنْ كَانَ ‏"</span><span class="arabic_sanad arabic">‏ ‏.‏</span></div>
</div>
